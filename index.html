Privacy Policy for the Boxo Quiz app

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who we are

Boxo Quiz makes puzzle videos, mazes, spelling games and squishy surprise-box episodes for kids and families. We publish them on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook.

The Boxo Quiz app is a private publishing tool. Boxo Quiz uses it only to post our own videos, images and comments to our own Facebook Page and our own Instagram account, and to read the performance of those posts.

What the app does not do

The app has no sign-up and no public users. Nobody outside Boxo Quiz logs in to it.

  • It does not collect or keep personal information about people who watch, like or comment on our posts.
  • It does not collect any information from children.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone.
  • It does not use advertising trackers or build profiles of viewers.

What the app uses

  • Access to our own accounts. A Meta access token for the Boxo Quiz Facebook Page and Instagram account, kept privately on Boxo Quiz's own computers and never published.
  • Our own content. The videos, images, captions and comments we create and choose to post.
  • Post results. Counts such as views, likes and comments on our own posts, which Meta provides to account owners. We use them only to decide what to make next.

Comments

Many of our videos are puzzles, and viewers type their guess in the comments. The next day we post the answer as a comment on our own post.

Just before posting the answer, the app reads the comments on that one post to count how many there are and how many named the right answer, so our reply can say, for example, "78 of you got it right!". Only those two numbers are used. The app does not save comments, names or usernames, and does not reply to or message individual viewers.

Your comment itself is handled by Facebook or Instagram under their own privacy policies.

Children

Our videos are made for families with children aged 4 to 10, and are meant to be watched with a parent or carer. The app collects no information from children, and we never ask children for personal details.

Data deletion

Because the app stores no information about viewers, there is nothing about you to delete. If you would still like us to remove something, such as one of your comments under our posts, send a message to the Boxo Quiz Facebook Page and we will act on it within 30 days.

Terms of use

The Boxo Quiz app is used only by Boxo Quiz to publish its own content. It offers no service to the public, so there is nothing to sign up for or agree to. Our videos are provided as entertainment for families, free to watch on each platform under that platform's own terms.

Changes

If the app ever starts to handle new kinds of data, we will update this page first and change the date at the top.

Contact

Message the Boxo Quiz Facebook Page, or reach us through the Boxo Quiz channel on YouTube (@boxoquiz).
